Export for Fluxygen

Makes a zip that Fluxygen imports: one file per Flow, with its Connections, Messages, Routes and Variables. The Flows go in the groupless group; in Fluxygen you can move them into groups of your own. Placeholders such as @{key} stay as they are.

Not included: Brokers, Queues and Topics, Certificates and users. Create them again in Fluxygen.
